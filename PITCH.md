ReportSathi: Telugu Voice-to-Complaint Draft for Cyber-Fraud Victims

Problem
When people face cyber fraud, they must give a clear written account to report it. Many find it hard to write that account in English, especially while distressed. Speaking in their own language is easier, but voice input alone does not produce an official-ready report.

Solution
ReportSathi helps a person describe the incident in Telugu and turns that description into a structured complaint draft. The user uploads a short Telugu audio clip, or types their account if they prefer. Gnani Prisma converts the speech into text, which the user reviews and corrects. They then select the incident type and whether money was transferred, and the app creates a copyable draft for the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal.

How Gnani AI is used
Gnani Prisma provides the Telugu speech-to-text step. It is the core of the voice workflow, so the app depends on it for the main input path.

Safety design

Urgent guidance appears first: call 1930 and contact the bank immediately if money or credentials may be at risk.
The app does not detect scams or decide whether a call was fraudulent.
It does not file reports on anyone’s behalf.
It does not store bank details, OTPs, passwords, Aadhaar numbers, or recordings.
Users must confirm that uploaded audio is their own or synthetic practice material.

Tested so far
One Telugu test clip was transcribed successfully. A second clip returned no transcript, so the app includes a manual fallback where users type their description and still create a draft. Copying the draft has been tested.

Limitations
Speech recognition can fail on unclear or long audio. The app supports only Telugu for now. It produces a draft, not an official submission.

Next steps
Improve audio handling, add more Indian languages, and refine the draft format based on feedback from people who have reported cyber fraud.
