TELUGU VOICE-TO-COMPLAINT DRAFT

ReportSathi

Describe a suspected cyber-fraud incident in Telugu. Review the transcript, then create a clear complaint draft.

Urgent: If money was transferred or credentials were shared, contact your bank and call 1930 immediately. You can also use the official National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal. This tool does not file a complaint or verify whether a call was fraudulent.

1. Upload a short Telugu recording

Use your own synthetic practice example only. Supported: WAV, MP3, OGG, FLAC, AAC, M4A. Keep it under 30 seconds. The audio is sent to Gnani for transcription and is not saved by this app.

2. Review and prepare the complaint draft

Please correct the transcript. If transcription fails, type the incident description yourself and continue. ReportSathi does not automatically decide what happened.

Privacy: Do not enter OTPs, PINs, passwords, bank-account numbers, Aadhaar numbers, or recordings of other people without consent.
